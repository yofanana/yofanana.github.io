Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

YANG YUANFANG ("we") explains below how the iPhone app "MichiTabi" (駅たび, "the App") handles information.

1. Information the App handles and where it is stored

The App has no account or sign-in, and we operate no server for it. The following information is stored only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Your Michi-no-Eki records (visited / want to go, visit dates, how each visit was confirmed)
  • Notes, ratings, travel journal entries (text, photos, stickers) and earned badges
  • Your display name and icon image
  • Settings (arrival distance, display language, etc.)

Deleting the App deletes this information from your device.

2. Location

The App uses your iPhone's location only while you are using the App ("While Using the App"), in order to:

  • show Michi-no-Eki near you, and
  • confirm that you have arrived at a Michi-no-Eki. Your distance to the station and the accuracy of the location fix are evaluated on the device.

Location data is used for calculations on your device only. It is not sent to us or to any third party. To show nearby stations next time, the App saves an approximate position, rounded to about 1 km, on your device. The App does not collect location in the background. You can change location access at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

3. Photos

  • Photos are chosen with the iOS photo picker. The App reads only the photos you select.
  • "Verify with GPS Photo" reads the location and date stored in the selected photo on your device to check whether it was taken near the station. The photo is not uploaded.
  • The App asks for add-only access to Photos only when you save a share image.

4. Backup files

Backup files (.michitabi) are created on your device. You choose where to save them (Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, etc.), and they are then handled under that service's terms.

5. Network connections

Destination Purpose Main information sent
Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org), websites of MLIT regional bureaus Displaying station photos Information needed for the connection (such as your IP address and standard request information), plus the App's name and version
Apple (App Store) In-app purchases and restore Handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy
Google (Google Mobile Ads SDK / User Messaging Platform) Showing ads and asking for ad consent See section 6

When you choose actions such as "Get Directions", the App opens Apple Maps or Google Maps with the station's name and location. Your own location is not passed. External websites you open are governed by their own policies.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows banner ads from Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC) on the Home screen and on station detail screens.

  • Data collected by the ads SDK: According to Google's published disclosure, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

    • your IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
    • crash logs and performance data;
    • a device identifier (the advertising identifier, or an app- or developer-scoped identifier);
    • advertising data, such as the ads you have seen;
    • other product interactions.

    Google uses this information to deliver, measure, analyze and improve ads. See Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

  • Tracking permission: The App does not ask for App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission, so the device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not provided to Google.

  • Consent: Where consent is legally required, such as in the EEA and the UK, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for consent before ads are loaded. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → "Ad Privacy Settings". This item appears only where it is required.
  • Ad partners: Where consent is required, information may also be shared, depending on your choices, with the ad technology providers chosen by Google that are listed in the consent form. You can see the list and their purposes from the form's options.
  • When ads are not shown: If you have purchased "Remove Ads" or a "Support MichiTabi" item, the App does not show ads, does not show the consent form and does not initialize the ads SDK. Ads are also not shown if the App cannot confirm your purchase status on the device.

7. In-app purchases

Payments are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details or Apple ID. The App uses Apple's StoreKit on your device only to check whether you have made a purchase.

8. Sharing with third parties

We do not provide your information to third parties. The connections described in sections 5 and 6, including sharing with ad partners based on your consent, are handled by each provider under its own policies.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

10. Changes

We will post changes on this page. We will also announce important changes in the App's update notes or by similar means.

11. Contact
