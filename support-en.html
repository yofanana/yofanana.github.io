MichiTabi Support

MichiTabi (駅たび) is an iPhone app for finding Michi-no-Eki roadside stations across Japan on a map and keeping a record of the ones you visit.

  • Device: iPhone (iOS 18.0 or later)
  • Languages: Japanese, English, Simplified Chinese

FAQ

Where does the station information come from? The list of stations (names and locations) is based on the Michi-no-Eki list published by Japan's Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (MLIT). Positions come mainly from Wikidata coordinates; some stations use a representative point of the municipality. Photos are mainly from Wikimedia Commons, and each photo shows its author and license. The App does not include opening hours, detailed facilities or the latest news. Please check each station's official information before you go.

How do I mark a station as visited? On a station's page, tap "Verify with My Location" to confirm that you are near the station. When the location fix is accurate enough three times in a row, the station is marked as visited. You can also use "Verify with GPS Photo". Stations you visited before can be added with "Add a past visit"; these are labeled "Self-reported".

Arrival can't be confirmed.

  • In Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services, set MichiTabi to "While Using the App".
  • Turn on "Precise Location".
  • Go outdoors with a clear view of the sky.

If arrival isn't confirmed within about 60 seconds, the App says so. Tap "Check Again" to retry. Some stations without precise location data can't be confirmed.

How do I remove ads? Buy "Remove Ads" (one-time purchase) or a "Support MichiTabi" item in Settings. These are not subscriptions.

My purchase isn't restored after changing or reinstalling. Sign in with the same Apple ID and tap "Restore Purchase" in Settings. If the App says it couldn't check your purchases, please try again later.

Product information doesn't load. Check your connection and tap "Reload". The App also tries again when the connection returns or when you reopen it.

How do I change my ad consent? In regions where consent is required (EEA, UK, etc.), use "Ad Privacy Settings" in Settings.

How do I back up my records or move them to another iPhone? Create a backup file in Settings → Data (Backup & Restore) and save it to Files, iCloud Drive or another location. Restore it from the same screen on the new device. Restoring merges with your current records and does not delete any. Records are not stored on a server, so always create a backup before deleting the App.

How do I delete my records? Individual visits can be deleted from a station's page. To delete everything, delete the App, along with any backup files you created.

Can I use this for official stamp rallies? No. Records in this App are not related to official stamp rallies.
